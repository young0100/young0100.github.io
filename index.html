Q01

Who is this for?

Chinese companies entering Korea. I also work with Korean brands heading into China. If you need the entity, partners, QC or counterfeit control set up and left running, that is the work.

Q02

What do you set up for a China-to-Korea entry?

I map the Korea path (entity, licensing, channel), sign Korea-side distributors and retail partners, and tie China-side supply QC to the claims trail. When fakes are undercutting you abroad, I add counterfeit monitoring and takedowns.

Q03

Do you only advise, or do you run it?

I set it up, then stay until it earns and can run without me. The sequence is map → set up → run and hand over.

Q04

What results can you point to?

From past work (company names withheld): USD $1M+ consumer-brand overseas revenue; 10,000+ counterfeit listings removed; USD $1.3M+ local-government subsidy secured on China-side advisory; Korea entry strategy and local partner network for a ~USD $200M+-revenue global consumer company.

Q05

Are you a law firm or tax firm?

No. I own entity setup, partner and distributor deals, QC and claims, counterfeit takedowns, and local-government negotiation. Licensed legal and tax are brought in when the work needs them.

Q06

Yes. Same operator model on the China side: entities, partners, QC, and counterfeit control, left running.
